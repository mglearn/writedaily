TEKS Alignment

Daily Writing a Day is aligned to the writing and oral-language strands of the Texas English Language Arts and Reading TEKS (19 TAC Chapter 110, Subchapters A and B), adopted in 2017.

Codes are aligned to, not reproduced from, the official standards. K–5 share one strand numbering and 6–8 shift down by one, but sub-letters can vary slightly by grade. Confirm against your grade's chapter (§110.2–§110.7 for K–5, §110.22–§110.24 for 6–8) before adopting.

Where the strands show up

In K–5, the writing process is strand (11), genres are (12), and inquiry and research is (13). In 6–8, the same strands are (10), (11), and (12). Oral language is strand (1) in every grade.

(1) Oral language
Students listen actively, speak clearly, and work with others. Fix It is done out loud: students explain each fix to a partner and the class.
(11)(A) / (10)(A) Plan
Generate ideas and plan a draft for a chosen genre and purpose (K–2: through drawing and discussion).
(11)(B) / (10)(B) Draft
Develop drafts into a focused, structured, coherent piece with an engaging idea and details.
(11)(C) / (10)(C) Revise
Revise for clarity, word choice, and sentence structure by adding, deleting, combining, and rearranging.
(11)(D) / (10)(D) Edit
Edit drafts using standard English conventions: complete sentences, verbs, nouns and pronouns, modifiers, conjunctions, capitalization, punctuation, and spelling. This is the daily Fix It focus.
(11)(E) / (10)(E) Publish
Publish or share writing for an appropriate audience. This is Friday's share.
(12) / (11) Genres
(A) literary texts: personal narrative, fiction, poetry. (B) informational texts, including procedural writing. (C) argumentative texts: opinion essays in 3–5, argumentative essays in 6–8; correspondence in K–2. (D) correspondence such as letters in 3–8.
(13) / (12) Inquiry and research
Generate questions, find and record information from sources, and share what was learned. Week 33 is the research week.
