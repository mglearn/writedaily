Daily Writing a Day

One editing sentence and one writing prompt for every school day, for grades K–2, 3–5, and 6–8. About 10 minutes, with no prep and no devices.

Week 1 starts on your first day; each following school week gets the next week of writing. Saved in this browser for both the calendar and the day pages.

How a week works. Monday plan, Tuesday draft, Wednesday revise, Thursday quick-write, Friday edit and share. Every prompt also works on its own, so you can skip a day.

Standards. Codes refer to the Texas English Language Arts and Reading TEKS (19 TAC §110.2–110.24). In K–5, the writing process is strand (11), genres are (12), and inquiry is (13). In 6–8 they are (10), (11), and (12). Fix It practices oral language (1) and editing for conventions. Sub-letters can vary slightly by grade, so check your grade's chapter.
